# MediaPrune Support

MediaPrune helps you review and organize photos and videos on your iPhone.

## Contact

Email: [support@passmate.jp](mailto:support@passmate.jp)

When contacting support, include:

- A short description of the issue
- Your MediaPrune version and build number
- Your iPhone model and iOS version
- The steps that led to the issue

Do not attach personal photos or videos unless you choose to do so. MediaPrune processes your media on your device and does not upload it to a developer server.

## Purchases

Use **Restore Purchases** in MediaPrune to restore eligible MediaPrune Pro access. Manage or cancel Monthly and Yearly subscriptions in your Apple Account subscription settings.
